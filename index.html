How fast will I see results?

Missed-call text-back typically starts recovering leads within days of installation. Reviews and search visibility compound over weeks to months. We'll show you leading indicators in your first monthly report — and we'll be honest about what's working and what isn't.

No. The system works with the phone number and tools you already use. We handle the technical setup — you keep doing your job.

I already have a receptionist. Do I still need this?

Your receptionist can't answer at 11 PM, on Sundays, or while on another call. LeadLock covers every gap — after hours, overlaps, lunch breaks — so no lead ever waits. Think of it as backup that never sleeps, not a replacement.

Am I locked into a contract?

No. Every plan is month-to-month. Cancel anytime — no cancellation fees, no guilt trip. We keep clients by delivering results, not paperwork.

Is the AI receptionist legal? What about robocall laws?

Yes — and this is exactly why you want a specialist. Our system is built TCPA-compliant from day one: proper consent flows, opt-outs on every automated message, and AI-disclosure where required (some states now fine AI calls that don't disclose). Compliance isn't an add-on; it's the foundation.

More customers now ask ChatGPT, Siri, or Alexa for "a roofer near me" instead of Googling. We structure your business information so AI assistants can find, trust, and recommend you — on top of classic local SEO.
